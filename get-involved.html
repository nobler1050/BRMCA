GET INVOLVED

Volunteering is a wonderful way to share your time and talents! Below you will find various areas of the BRMCA where you can get involved.

County Planning

Maintain dialogue with county officials in Clarke and Loudoun. These committees work to keep association members informed on issues and legislation that affect our community.

Land Use / Commercialization

Informs members of land use regulations and conservation efforts. The committee aims to ensure residents abide by these regulations to preserve the natural beauty of the mountain.

Road Clean-Up

Each spring and fall, members work together to remove litter along Rt. 601. Once the work is completed, neighbors meet for coffee and donuts!

Social

Organizes potluck dinners, picnics, and refreshments for general board meetings. Activities include baking contests, resident art displays, and live music.
