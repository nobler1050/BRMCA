Get Involved

Blue Ridge Mountain Civic Association is an all-volunteer organization, which means we need volunteers.

Run for an office at our annual meeting, join a committee, or just raise your hand to get more involved.

Communications

Help keep BRMCA members up to date through our website and periodic emails.

County Planning

Maintain dialog with county officials and other organizations aligned with the BRMCA mission and work with the Communications team to keep members informed.

Firewise and Preparedness

Help coordinate the annual brush removal as well as the Wildfire Evacuation Plan.

Historical

Research and share the history of our portion of the Blue Ridge Mountain.

Land Use / Commercialization

Investigate issues related to proposed land use by individuals and businesses and work with the Communications team to keep members informed.

Membership

Work to increase awareness of BRMCA and boost our numbers.

Adopt-a-Highway

Our members work to remove litter along Blue Ridge Mountain Road. Volunteers coordinate with VDOT and share coffee and donuts once the work is done.

Social

Help organize potluck dinners, picnics, and refreshments for membership meetings, plus bring new ideas. We’ve had baking contests, art displays, and live music in the past.
