Membership

Support Your Mountain Community

The Blue Ridge Mountain Civic Association (BRMCA) focuses on safeguarding the natural environment of the Blue Ridge and preserving the vital resources that make our mountain home special.

By becoming a member, you help us sustain the mountain's unique character and ensure the tranquil solitude we all cherish. Growing our membership strengthens our voice and our ability to advocate for the community's interests.

Membership Benefits

  • Voting rights on association matters and leadership elections.
  • Access to community events and the annual potluck picnic.
  • Regular updates on important community news and alerts.
  • Opportunities to volunteer and participate in conservation efforts.
