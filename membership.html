Membership

Our organization is an effective advocate for the preservation and protection of our mountain, but only if we have a thriving membership. What can you do? Glad you asked.

  1. Awareness — Be aware of and understand the planning, zoning, commercialization, and other issues that might affect our way of life.
  2. Influence — Take action when necessary, whether that’s posting a sign, attending a board of supervisors meeting, or calling state and local officials.
  3. Involvement — Connect with your neighbors through BRMCA activities that range from membership meetings (typically in April and October), road cleanup, and social events.

It all starts with you signing up.

Thanks to a generous donation from Wildwood Landscape in Purcellville, we’ve been able to waive the cost of your annual membership for the year ahead … though we will be asking for contributions during our end-of-the-year donation drive.
