Firewise USA®

The Firewise program, made possible by a grant from the Virginia Department of Forestry, works to reduce damage to homes in the event of a wildfire in high-risk communities like ours.

At the appointed time, participants clear downed branches — potential embers — from around their homes and stack the brush neatly along Blue Ridge Mountain Road. BRMCA typically uses our grant funds to hire a professional tree crew, along with a wood chipper, to clear the stacks.

If you choose to participate, please track your progress on the form below.

For more information on the Firewise program, visit the National Fire Protection Association website or email BRMCA’s Mick Newman at mick@mandjnewman.com for details.
