Ready, Set, Flee: Your Wildfire Evacuation Plan

Please take the time to read this Fire evacuation plan developed by BRMCA. It contains vital information about preparation planning and steps to take in the event of a wildfire on our ridge.

1. Introduction

The Blue Ridge Mountain Civic Association (BRMCA) offers this plan to residents living along both sides of Blue Ridge Mountain and Raven Rocks Roads. It was developed in consultation with officials from Loudoun and Clarke Counties as well as businesses/employers.

While Clarke County and the Mountain Overlay district of Loudoun County are rated as having a moderate risk of wildfire on a national scale, within the state of Virginia our ridge is in the 90th percentile for wildfire risk. A May 2025 assessment by the Virginia Department of Forestry rated the BRMCA community area as having a high risk (143 rating). That risk goes up considerably in periods of drought and the presence of additional visitors.

2. Be Prepared

a. Sign up for CodeRED
CodeRED is the warning application used by Loudoun and Clarke Counties to alert areas of emergencies. On your smartphone download the CodeRED app (Onsolve is the provider).

b. Harden your home
While it's not possible to fireproof a forest, you can significantly reduce wildfire risk by creating defensible space around your home. Visit nfpa.org for in-depth information.

c. If you have to leave, have a plan for what to take
Prepare a "Go Bag" – an emergency evacuation kit containing items you may need to sustain you and your family short term. Helpful links:
• Ready for Wildfire - Supply Kit
• FEMA - Safeguard Documents

FireWise On the Mountain

Protect the mountain from potential forest fires by joining our annual Firewise program. Historically, we have used grant funds from the Virginia Department of Forestry to hire a professional tree crew for several days, along with a wood chipper/shredder.

We anticipate receiving a grant for 2026. Please begin now to keep track of your hours spent and out-of-pocket costs on Firewise activities. VDoF now requires dates of these activities and expenditures for audit purposes.
