Welcome to the Mountain

The officers and directors of the Blue Ridge Mountain Civic Association welcome you to the community.

Our woodland surroundings offer tranquil solitude for all who live here, but we all share a common goal: to maintain the quality of life on the mountain.

Our organization was established in 2003 with a mission to preserve the unspoiled beauty of the mountain and protect the resources of forest, water, and wildlife so residents can enjoy the privacy and peacefulness.

This site is a resource for newcomers and longtime residents alike, offering information on upcoming events, important news, and volunteer opportunities, all with an aim to preserve and protect mountain life.

We encourage your questions, membership, and participation, strengthening our ability to sustain the mountain.
