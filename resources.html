Loudoun Express Request (LEx)

LEx is a citizen request system that lets members of the public submit requests for service and report concerns to Loudoun County government via the web or mobile app. Mobile apps are available for iOS and Android.

  • Western Loudoun Recycling Center: 38159 Colonial Highway, Hamilton. 571-252-1000.
  • Clarke County Quarry Road Convenience Center: Open Friday–Monday with restricted hours; does not accept glass or certain plastics.

Outdoor Burning Restrictions

Virginia law restricts outdoor burning from February 15 through April 30. Burning is only permitted between 4 PM and midnight during this period.
