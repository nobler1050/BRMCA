Mountain Rules & Living

Before You Start an Outdoor Fire…

  • Clarke County residents must call the Emergency Communications Center at 📞 540-955-1234 before burning.
  • Loudoun County residents must call the Emergency Communications Center at 📞 703-777-0637 before burning.
Virginia law restricts outdoor burning from February 15 through April 30 when high winds are most prevalent during daytime hours. Outdoor burning is only permitted between 4 PM and midnight during this 11-week period.

Mountain Neighbors & Wildlife

Bear’s Den — Appalachian Trail

Owned by the Appalachian Trail Conservancy and operated by the Potomac Appalachian Trail Club, Bear’s Den provides a gateway to the “rollercoaster” section of the AT. Bear’s Den Lodge, a historic stone mansion built in 1933, overlooks the Shenandoah Valley approximately 150 yards from the trail.

Visit Bear’s Den Center

Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA)

The Mount Weather Emergency Operations Center is a government command facility located along Blueridge Mountain Rd., used as the center of operations for the Federal Emergency Management Agency.
