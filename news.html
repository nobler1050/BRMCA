Annual Pot Luck Picnic

The BRMCA is hosting its annual pot luck picnic for residents along Route 601 and connected roads!

When: May 16, between 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM

Where: 20282 Blue Ridge Mountain Rd, Paris, VA

Details:

  • Parking: Limited to 30 cars. Please carpool if possible!
  • Food: BRMCA will provide hot dogs, hamburgers, and water.
  • What to Bring: Please bring a dish or dessert to share, and your own chairs or blankets.
  • RSVP: Please email Kim Larson at kllkimmer@comcast.net by May 9.
