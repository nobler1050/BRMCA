Our Background

Wildwood Landscape is a distinct design and construction firm serving the DC Metropolitan area since 2005. We believe that landscaping is a fine art and should be practiced with the same care, thought and skill as other high art forms. Our designs have been recognized by both The Leesburg Flower and Garden Festival (7 consecutive wins) and The Washington Home and Garden Show (7 awards including an award for having performed better than any other company in the 51 year history of the show). The Landscape Contractors Association presented Wildwood Landscape their Distinction Award for Excellence in Landscape in the category of Outdoor Living Area Design/Build.

Jason Dengler, owner of Wildwood Landscape LLC, has over 25 years of Landscape design and installation experience. Jason is committed to your success and has a vested interest in the community in which he chose to start his business. Being a community-based company means that Wildwood Landscape will take great pride in our attention, care and tailored solutions that only a local, full service company can provide.
